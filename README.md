EmoCare: User Manual & Platform Overview(https://emocare-4-9is5.onrender.com/)
EmoCare is a web-based emotional wellness platform designed to act as your personal, 24/7 mental health companion. It combines an intuitive dashboard, private journaling, and conversational AI to help you track, understand, and improve your emotional well-being.

1. The Dashboard (Your Command Center)
When you log in, you are greeted by your personal dashboard. This is where you get a bird's-eye view of your mental health journey.

Mood Visualizations: The dashboard features interactive charts (powered by Recharts) that plot your mood over time. You can see trends, identify emotional triggers, and understand the rhythm of your well-being.
Quick Summaries: It provides a snapshot of your recent journal entries and AI interactions, giving you immediate insights without having to dig through your history.
2. The AI Companion (Your 24/7 Listener)
At the heart of EmoCare is the AI Chat interface.

Empathetic AI: You can converse with an AI trained to listen, validate your feelings, and offer supportive, non-judgmental responses at any time of day.
Guided Conversations: Whether you're feeling anxious, sad, or just need to vent, the AI companion helps you process your emotions constructively.
3. Private Journaling (Your Safe Space)
Daily Reflections: A dedicated space to write down your thoughts, fears, and triumphs.
Mood Tagging: When you write an entry, you can tag it with your current mood. This data feeds directly into your dashboard charts, seamlessly connecting your thoughts with your emotional data.
Complete Privacy: Your entries are secured behind authenticated logins (using JWT and Google Auth) and safely stored in the database, ensuring your deepest thoughts remain private.
4. The Experience (Beautiful & Seamless)
The platform is built with a breathtaking, modern design (using Tailwind CSS and Framer Motion). From the moment you land on the page, the smooth animations, calming color palettes, and responsive layout are designed to reduce anxiety and provide a soothing user experience.

In short, EmoCare isn't just an app; it's a closed-loop system for emotional growth. You chat to relieve immediate stress, journal to reflect deeper, and view your dashboard to learn about your long-term emotional patterns.
